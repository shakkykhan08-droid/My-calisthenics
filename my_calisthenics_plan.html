Safety reminders

Warm up before training. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms. Don't practise advanced holds to failure. Use only stable, secure equipment; never use an unstable chair or table for rows or support holds. Rest and recover—don't force every session to fill all 60 minutes. At your current level, build basic strength before attempting planche, front lever, muscle-up or human flag.
